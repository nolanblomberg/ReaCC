Unlock plug-in linking

What you’re unlocking: connect a ReaCC fader directly to a plug-in knob. The phone fader follows that knob and its automation, and moving the phone fader controls the plug-in. Your regular MIDI CC faders keep working without this setup.

Open ReaCC on your phone (one-time REAPER setup)

  1. In REAPER, open Options → Preferences → Control/OSC/web, click Add, and choose Web browser interface.
  2. In the web interface settings, open User pages… and select ReaCC.html.
  3. Use the displayed Access URL in a browser on your phone or tablet. Keep the device and computer on the same network.

Use optional DAW parameter mirroring

  1. Run the ReaCC installer once. It installs the bridge and registers the mirror script. On the next REAPER launch, the script adds CC Fader Bridge to Monitoring FX if needed. If REAPER was already open, restart it once.
  2. To link a fader, open a plug-in window and move the control you want to mirror. Audio playback is not required.

When ReaCC unlocks, tap Mirror DAW on the fader you want. It links to the last plug-in control you moved. The broken-link icon becomes connected and the label changes to “Mirroring.” Tap again to cancel learning or remove the link.

Waiting for the bridge and mirror script to respond…

If it stays locked, make sure REAPER has been restarted since installation, then move a plug-in control again. Regular MIDI CC control works even while mirroring is locked.
